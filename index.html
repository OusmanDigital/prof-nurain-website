The Story Behind the Name

About Prof Nurain

Nuraini Mohammad Usman, also known as Prof Nurain, is a Nigerian poet, story writer and teller, photographer, creative artist, essayist, and a proud feminist. His artistry flows through words, images, and emotions, blending creativity with conviction to tell stories that matter.

Born into and raised in the Muyawa family in Kano State, and brought up in Minna, Niger State, Nuraini's diverse background deeply influences his artistic vision and expression.

His interests span literature, visual storytelling, photography, and the relationship between technology and creativity. He uses creative expression to explore ideas, emotions, and human experiences.

Currently pursuing Software Engineering, Nuraini continues to develop his artistic voice while building his technical knowledge.

Creativity with Conviction

Words can express what the heart feels. Images can preserve what the eyes see. Stories can help people understand one another.

Prof Nurain brings together artistic expression, curiosity, and a vision for the future.

Educational Journey

Each stage of Nuraini's education has contributed to his development and ambitions.

01. Dayamas Model School

Began his educational journey with kindergarten and nursery education.

02. Better Treasure International School

Continued his education at the primary school level.

03. Al-Fawzu Azeem Academy

Attended junior secondary education, building the foundation for further academic growth.

04. Legend International School

Completed his junior and senior secondary studies at Legend International School.

05. Maryam Abacha American University of Niger

Nuraini is a 200-level Software Engineering student at Maryam Abacha American University of Niger (MAAUN), Maradi, Niger Republic.

His studies reflect his interest in technology and his desire to combine technical skills with creative thinking.

Looking Ahead

A Vision for the Future

Ambitious and driven by purpose, Nuraini dreams of becoming a lecturer in the future. He pursues this vision with passion, discipline, and the steadfast support of his parents.

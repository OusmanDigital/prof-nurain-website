The Person Behind the Art

Biography

A journey shaped by family, education, imagination, literature, and creative expression.

Nuraini Mohammad Usman, also known as Prof Nurain, is a Nigerian slithering poet, story writer and teller, photographer, creeping artist, essayist, and a proud feminist. His artistry flows through words, images, and emotions — blending creativity with conviction to tell stories that matter.

Born into and raised in the Muyawa family, in Kano State, and brought up in Minna, Niger State, Nuraini’s diverse background deeply influences his artistic vision and expression.

He began his educational journey at Dayamas Model School for his kindergarten and nursery education, continued to Better Treasure International School for his primary level, and then to Al-Fawzu Azeem Academy for his junior secondary education. Later, he moved to Legend International School, where he completed both his junior and senior secondary studies with excellence.

Currently, Nuraini is a 200-level Software Engineering student at Maryam Abacha American University of Niger Republic (MAAUN), Maradi. He beautifully bridges the world of art and technology, embodying creativity in both thought and practice.

Ambitious and driven by purpose, Nuraini dreams of becoming a lecturer in the future — a vision he pursues with passion, discipline, and the steadfast support of his parents. His story stands as a testament to talent, resilience, and the power of creativity to transform lives.
